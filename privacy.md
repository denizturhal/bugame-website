# Privacy Policy — Color Merge Sort

**Last updated: November 12, 2026**

Color Merge Sort is developed and published by Deniz Turhal, operating under the name Bugame ("we," "us," or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you play Color Merge Sort (the "Game").

## 1. Information We Collect

### Gameplay Information

We may collect information about your interactions with the Game, including:

- Levels started, completed, or failed.
- Time spent playing and completing levels.
- Game progression and session activity.
- Use of boosters, rewards, and other gameplay features.

We use this information to understand player experiences, improve game balance, and enhance the Game.

### Device and Technical Information

Our service providers may collect technical information, including:

- Device type, operating system, and application version.
- IP address and approximate location derived from your IP address.
- Installation identifiers and other device identifiers.
- Advertising identifiers, where permitted by applicable law and your device permissions.

### Game Progress

Your game progress, coins, lives, boosters, settings, and other gameplay information may be stored locally on your device to allow you to continue playing.

The Game also uses Unity services to retrieve game settings, levels, and downloadable content.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide and maintain the Game.
- Save and manage gameplay progress.
- Improve game performance, level design, and player experience.
- Deliver and measure advertisements.
- Provide rewarded advertisements and in-game rewards.
- Manage privacy preferences and comply with applicable legal obligations.
- Prevent fraud, abuse, and technical issues.
- Respond to support and privacy requests.

## 3. Analytics

The Game uses Unity Analytics to understand how players interact with the Game.

Analytics information may include gameplay events, session activity, installation identifiers, and technical device information.

Where legally required, analytics collection is subject to your consent. Advertising consent and analytics consent are treated separately.

For more information about Unity's data practices, please visit:

https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

## 4. Advertising

The Game may display interstitial advertisements and optional rewarded advertisements.

We use Unity LevelPlay to manage advertising through participating advertising networks, which may include Unity Ads and ironSource services.

Advertising partners may process device information, IP addresses, advertising identifiers, and advertisement interaction data to deliver advertisements, measure performance, and prevent fraud.

Personalized advertising is subject to applicable privacy requirements and user preferences.

On iOS, the Game may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. You may decline this permission and continue playing the Game. Declining tracking permission does not necessarily prevent advertisements from being displayed.

You can manage tracking permissions through your device's settings.

Unity's privacy policy:

https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

## 5. Privacy Preferences and Consent

We use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to help manage privacy preferences and consent requirements.

Depending on your region, you may be presented with privacy choices concerning advertising, data processing, and other applicable privacy rights.

You can manage or withdraw applicable consent through the privacy options available in the Game's settings.

Google's privacy policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Children's Privacy

Color Merge Sort is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 in violation of applicable children's privacy laws.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us. We will investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required.

## 7. Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers that support the Game's operation, analytics, advertising, privacy management, and technical infrastructure.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

We do not sell personal information for monetary payment. However, certain advertising-related disclosures may be considered selling, sharing, or targeted advertising under applicable privacy laws.

Where applicable, users may exercise their rights to opt out of such processing through available privacy controls or by contacting us.

## 8. International Data Transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence.

Where required, appropriate legal safeguards are used for international data transfers.

## 9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Game, improve its functionality, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Information processed by third-party service providers may be retained according to their applicable retention policies.

You may contact us to request deletion of personal information associated with your use of the Game.

Deleting the Game from your device may remove locally stored game progress, but it does not necessarily delete information previously processed by our service providers.

## 10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access personal information held about you.
- Request correction or deletion of personal information.
- Withdraw consent.
